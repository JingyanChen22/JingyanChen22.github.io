Research projects

Computational linguistics, with a focus on the fairness and efficiency of language models.

Master’s thesis: Pronominal Gender Bias in LLMs with Language Adapters

Saarland UniversityJun 2023 – Feb 2024

Language adapters are portable and parameter-efficient, but their gender bias is under-explored. I built a Chinese Winogender dataset (120 templates, 720 sentences) parallel to the English one, and compared base, adapter and fully fine-tuned versions of mBERT, XLM-RoBERTa and mT5 on pronoun prediction and coreference resolution.

  • Multilingual models generally performed worse than monolingual ones.
  • Extra language-model training usually added more gender bias without reliably improving performance.
  • Reliable evaluation depends on training data, task, prompt quality and automated bias measurement.

Seminar project: Parameter-efficient fine-tuning

Saarland UniversityFeb 2023 – Apr 2023

Compared full fine-tuning with adapters and prefix-tuning on XLM-RoBERTa for cross-lingual question answering (XQuAD) and natural language inference (XNLI). Full fine-tuning was slightly ahead, while lightweight methods stayed comparable and far cheaper.

Research Assistant: Modeling and measuring information density

Spoken Language Systems group (LSV), Saarland UniversityJan 2023 – Jun 2023

Trained four tokenizers (WordLevel, BPE, WordPiece, Unigram) and LSTM language models on the Royal Society Corpus, comparing word surprisal across tokenization methods as groundwork for psycholinguistic analysis.
